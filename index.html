About Me

Kia ora! My name is Caelan Reader-Scott, I'm a beginning writer who's looking for a place to post my work for others to read and enjoy. I have a background in English and Creative Writing from the University of Alberta. My usual bread and butter is speculative fiction and poetry but I occaisonally dabble in non-fiction such as game reviews. Please have a look around and read my work, some of which was made for past courses.
